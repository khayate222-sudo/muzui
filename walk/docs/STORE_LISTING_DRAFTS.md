## English

**Name:** WALK

**Short description:** Find a video, a little world, and someone who shares your interests.

Start at a curious station and wander into worlds of art, games, music and everyday discoveries. Find a video, watch beside the miniature world or settle into your own window-side room.

See how many people have the same post open. Names and avatars appear according to each person's sharing choice. Save something you love, react, and connect through requests that both people approve.

For supported WALK-hosted videos, invite a friend to watch in sync. Joining is always a choice. External videos use their official players or source links; availability, login and playback conditions depend on the provider. External players do not support WALK synchronized viewing.

Share your own writing, images or short videos. Keep a private daily activity journal, with selected activities shared only when you choose. Activities are self-reported and do not use GPS tracking.

WALK's core experience is free. Paid features are not enabled in this version.
